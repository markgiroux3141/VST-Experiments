# Pultec HLF-3C sources

The program and sound effects filter (docs/EQ_PLAN.md EQ1; MIX_INSERTS_PLAN.md MX2).
Values in `config/circuits/pultec_hlf3c.yaml` (GENERATED by `scripts/make_pultec_hlf3c.py`)
cite these as `man:` (the brochure), `dwg:` (the hand-drawn schematic's HLF-3C table),
`dwg-hlf3:` (the same sheet's drawing of the older HLF-3) or `derived:`.

## How the values were read

- The HLF-3C table gives capacitances; its coils only by wire colour. Its LF positions are
  the HLF-3's (50 ... 2000 cps), so L-1's taps are taken from the HLF-3's drawing
  (`dwg-hlf3:`). Its HF positions 1.5 ... 12 kc are on the HLF-3's HF toroids too; 15 kc
  is new, and its tap is derived for a constant-K section at the drawing's 500 ohm:
  R / (2 pi f) = 5.31 mH per half.
- Both sections check against constant-K design at 500 ohm (a T section terminated in
  its R is a 3rd-order Butterworth: GroupDIY's "Butterworth Tee", 18 dB / octave). The LF
  taps are R / (4 pi f) to within 2% (800 mH at 50 cps: 796); the HF taps R / (2 pi f)
  (52 mH at 1.5 kc: 53); the HLF-3C's C-3 is 1 / (pi R f) within ~6% except 5 kc
  (0.1066 uF against 0.127). The HLF-3C's C-1 / C-2 run up to 20% below 1 / (2 pi R f)
  at 50 cps and 1-2 kc (5.13 uF against 6.37), so those positions cut a little higher;
  the HLF-3's drawing has 6.15 uF at 50 cps (0.15 always in plus 6.0).
- The values 5.13, 4.13, ..., 0.13 are a 0.13 uF always in plus the position's
  (the HLF-3's 0.15 plus 6.0, 4.0, ... is the same scheme); the circuit uses the totals.

## What isn't known

- **The coils' resistance and losses.** Not printed. The coil resistance follows the
  EQP-1A's tapped toroid (a vendor's 14 ohm at its first tap, rising as sqrt(L):
  `data/schematics/pultec_eqp1a/README.md`), provisional.
- **The core material.** "Shielded toroid coils"; filter toroids of the period were
  molybdenum permalloy powder (a distributed gap, nearly linear to high flux): the coils
  are modelled linear. Whether they saturate at +28 dBm is an open option (docs/EQ_PLAN.md).
- **The source and load.** "500-600 ohms": the circuit file's source and load are 600 ohm
  (the studio line), the design impedance 500. GroupDIY builders report a small bump at
  the knee with a lighter load (web: https://groupdiy.com/threads/pultec-hlf3c-correct-source-and-load-impedance.67010/).

A modern clone's redraw (Bo Deadly's, GroupDIY thread 67010, on Audio Maintenance's
VTA1430 / VTA1421 inductors) uses other taps; it isn't a source for the original's values.
