## Readings (2026-11-10, from the 400 dpi renders)

**The pre stage** (BA283's TR4-TR6 between pins N-V; BA284's T1-T3 identical, its
designators in brackets): U -> C8 10u [C1] -> TR4's base. R9 120k [R1] U to V. TR4 (BC184C)
common emitter: collector load R12 47k [R4] to a node held by C12 22u [C5] to V and R11 33k
[R3] to N (+24 V); C9 100p [C2] collector to base, C11 680p [C4] collector to V, C10 1500p
[C3] emitter to base. TR5 (BC184C) common emitter, base on TR4's collector: collector R13
5k1 [R5] to N; emitter R14 470 [R6] to node H, H via R15 1k5 [R7] to V and C13 125u [C6] to
V; R10 68k [R2] from H to TR4's base (DC bias). S -> C14 22u [C7] -> TR5's emitter, R16 10k
[R8] S to V. TR6 (BC184C) follower: collector N, base on TR5's collector, emitter -> C15 22u
[C8] -> P, R20 51k [R12] P to V, C16 1000p [C26] emitter to V. Feedback: R17 2k2 [R9] from
TR6's emitter to node F = TR4's emitter; R18 390 [R10] F to V; F -> C17 400u [C9] -> T, R19
1k8 [R11] T to V.

**In the module (EH10023):** T1 10468's secondary pin 7 to node 3 (S2a's wiper), the other
end (circle 3b) to the bus that is ground below R10 91 (R10 from the BA284's T to that bus: the
BA284's gain fixed). At -80 to -55 dBm S2a puts node 3 on the BA284's U; the BA284's output P
feeds the ladder R20 3k9, R21 3k3, R22 2k7, R11 2k7 to ground, and S2b picks P (-80 / -75 /
-70) or a tap (-65 / -60 / -55) onto node 6, the BA283's U (R38 18k between its S and U; R8
15k likewise on the BA284). S2c puts R29 120 (-80, -50) or R30 330 (-75, -45) from the
BA283's T to ground. At -50 to -20 the BA284 is bypassed: node 3 into node 6 directly (-50),
with a shunt (-45 R13 18k, -40 R14 12k) or through an L-pad (series R3 2k2 / R4 3k3 / R5 3k9 /
R6 4k3, shunt R15 3k9 / R16 1k8 / R17 1k / R18 510 at -35 / -30 / -25 / -20). Line
positions (+10 to -20) use T2 31267 and R9 / R24-R28 / R7 / R1 (not built).

**Checked by the labels:** with T1 1:2 (HI) the drawings' stage gains add to EK20033's dBm
labels (-70: 30.2 + 17.9 + 15.5 + 6 = 69.6 dB; -80: S2c's 120 ohm +10.5; -50: 6 + 28.4 + 15.5),
which is how S2c's wiper was read as grounded and the T bus below R10 as ground.

**The output stage** (EX/10283 TR1-TR3): L -> C1 10u -> R1 2k2 -> TR1's base; R2 56k from
RV1's wiper; C3 4700p base to emitter; TR1's collector R3 68k to M, C2 220p to its base;
emitter R4 1k2 to J, K (R44 1k5 + C5 100u in the module); feedback from the collector node B
through C4 80u and R5 3k3 || C5 330p to TR1's emitter. TR2 (BC184C) collector B, emitter to
TR3's base and R6 18k; TR3 (2N3055 / BDY61) collector B, emitter R7 47 to A, RV1 4k7 to J
(wiper to R2), C6 80u to M; C7 80u B to F, R8 33k. The LO1166's primary from M (B+ through
R56 12, C7 / C8 470u) to B; its secondary with R64 1k5 + C9 .01 to the balanced output.
