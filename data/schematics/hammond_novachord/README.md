# Hammond Novachord sources

The instrument of docs/NOVACHORD_PLAN.md. `config/circuits/novachord_note.yaml` and
`config/circuits/novachord.yaml` are GENERATED by `scripts/make_novachord.py`, whose values
cite these documents as `SM2 Fig. N` / `SM2 p.N`. The scans are not committed (cite, don't
copy): they are git-ignored here, listed with their hashes. `RESEARCH_NOTES.md` is the first
reading (2026-11-07), every value with its URL.

## Gaps (for the user)

1. **Fig. 18's CN3.** Printed ".009 TO .018" (read at 500 dpi, unambiguous); the lowest-octave
   divider divides in the model only near ten times that (the trim's sweep), where Fig. 16's
   range lands an octave down. A misprint, or the 6W7-G's model at +70 V on its plate is off?
   A restorer's measured value (Cirocco's notes, discretesynthesizers.com) would settle it.
2. **The dividers' adjustment resistors centre at 0.8-1.7 MEG** in the model, the manual's
   1.7-2.5 MEG (1.2-3.5 in extremes). The model's dividers idle at the manual's 0.2 mA but at a
   net bias of ~3.3 V, the manual's "about 6 volts": the 6W7-G's cutoff (RCA's -7 V at 100 V
   screen) is what the fit has. A measured 6W7-G (or the 6J7's curves) would refine it.
3. **Coil and resonator inductances** (the tuning coils, LN2-LN5) aren't printed anywhere found.
4. **The vibrato:** the reeds' rates and the depth (six reeds, ~7 Hz; no depth in cents found), and SMALL's first pole (docs/NOVACHORD_PLAN.md, Status, gap 3).
5. **The attack switch's resistor strings (RN52)** and the tone controls' resistors.
6. **The power amplifier's output transformer and the speakers** (Hammond's; the fields'
   speakers' make).
