Can you tell a pair apart at all? X is secretly A or B. p: the chance of at least that many hits by guessing (one-sided). JND k: the staircase's estimate (geometric mean of k at its reversals). k ≈ 1.5 means the real difference is just audible; k ≈ 6, preference answers on the pair are mostly noise. The anchor is the most different pair at k = 8: missing it means the setup or the attention is off.

Difference listening

What does the part change? The difference between a matched pair splits in two: its tone (T: what a fixed EQ / phase filter turning B into A explains) and its character (C: what no fixed filter explains: distortion, pumping, thump). Pick a pair and a part, then listen to the part on its own, turned up, and to A + k × the part. Everything plays at one loudness. Exaggerations are not units: training for the ear, never judgments.
