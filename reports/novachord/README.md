# The Novachord's reports

docs/NOVACHORD_PLAN.md; the circuits are `config/circuits/novachord_note.yaml` and
`novachord.yaml` (GENERATED by `scripts/make_novachord.py`).

- `study.json`: `scripts/novachord_study.py`: the vibrato on note A (NORMAL, SMALL, both: the
  reed's rate and swing, the pitch's mean and depth) and the chord sag (1, 5, 10, 20 keys at
  ATTACK 4: the control tubes' B+ and the output's level), at draft quality.
- `renders/`: `scripts/novachord_render.py`: `<name>.wav` (0 dBFS = the circuit's full scale:
  with AMP on, 1 Pa 1 m in front of the console; AMP off, 1 V of the preamplifier's line),
  `<name>.json` (settings, time taken, peak), `<name>.mid` for the demo.

Everything here is regenerated by those scripts; nothing is hand edited.
