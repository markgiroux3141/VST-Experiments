# The Clavinet D6, to listen to

Rendered by `scripts/clavinet_listen_set.py`: the strings from physics
(varimu/clavinet), the D6's electronics (config/circuits/clavinet_d6.yaml, the amp
provisional until the schematic is read). 0 dBFS = 2 V at the volume pot's wiper
(volume 0.8). The tone and pickup comparisons share one strings render each.

- `notes_keyboard.wav`: F1, E2, E3, E4, E5, E6 at mf, each held 1.5 s then released (the 3-semitone release and the yarn's kill) (peak 1.35 V)
- `velocity_e3.wav`: E3 at pp, mf, ff, each staccato then held (peak 0.60 V)
- `riff_pickups_AC_centre.wav`: the demo phrase, pickups AC_centre (MEDIUM + TREBLE) (peak 1.08 V)
- `riff_pickups_BC_bridge.wav`: the demo phrase, pickups BC_bridge (MEDIUM + TREBLE) (peak 1.72 V)
- `riff_pickups_AD_anti.wav`: the demo phrase, pickups AD_anti (MEDIUM + TREBLE) (peak 1.78 V)
- `riff_pickups_BD_both.wav`: the demo phrase, pickups BD_both (MEDIUM + TREBLE) (peak 1.40 V)
- `riff_tone_soft.wav`: the demo phrase, tone SOFT (both pickups in phase) (peak 0.35 V)
- `riff_tone_medium.wav`: the demo phrase, tone MEDIUM (both pickups in phase) (peak 0.67 V)
- `riff_tone_treble.wav`: the demo phrase, tone TREBLE (both pickups in phase) (peak 1.47 V)
- `riff_tone_brilliant.wav`: the demo phrase, tone BRILLIANT (both pickups in phase) (peak 0.67 V)
- `riff_tone_medium+treble.wav`: the demo phrase, tone MEDIUM+TREBLE (both pickups in phase) (peak 1.40 V)
- `riff_tone_all.wav`: the demo phrase, tone ALL (both pickups in phase) (peak 1.40 V)
- `riff_muted.wav`: the demo phrase with the mute bar fully on (peak 1.20 V)

What to listen for: the attack's click (the tangent's strike on the anvil), the
release's drop (three semitones, then gone), the bass's long sustain, the pickups'
comb (A+D thin, B+D full), the rockers' colours, the knock under the attack (CL4: the
board under the anvils, subtle). Not modelled yet: aftertouch bends in renders, the
amp's real schematic.
