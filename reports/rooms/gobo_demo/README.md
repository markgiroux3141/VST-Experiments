# A gobo between two players: a listening demo

Generated by `scripts/room_gobo_demo.py` (docs/ROOM_LOOP_PLAN.md R11). The lead vocal and an
acoustic guitar of David Tyo's "Long Way Home" (Cambridge-MT multitrack: educational use, never
committed) played 1.8 m apart, each on a clean small **omni** 0.3 m in front (an omni hears
the other player as well as its own, so the gobo is all the isolation there is). The gobo:
1.4 m wide, 1.8 m tall, 8 cm thick, heavy drapes, between them. Below 500 Hz the wave solver
bends the sound round it; above, the geometric model with the gobo's four edges diffracting.

| Folder | Room | Gobo |
|---|---|---|
| `open/` | the live room (7 x 5.5 x 3.2 m, furnished; RT ~0.8 s) | no |
| `gobo/` | the same | yes |
| `treated_open/` | the same room treated (drapes on the walls, carpet, acoustic-tile ceiling) | no |
| `treated_gobo/` | the same | yes |

In each folder: `vocal_spot.wav`, `guitar_spot.wav` (both players), and each player alone through
each mic: **`guitar_spot__vocal.wav` is the vocal's bleed into the guitar's mic**,
`vocal_spot__guitar.wav` the guitar's into the vocal's. A room's two folders share one gain
(its open guitar spot at -18 LUFS): the bleed's drop is heard as it is, don't re-level.

**Listen for:** `open/guitar_spot__vocal.wav` against `gobo/guitar_spot__vocal.wav`, then the
same pair in `treated_*`. The bleed's change with the gobo, per octave (`bleed.json`):

| Octave (Hz) | 63 | 125 | 250 | 500 | 1k | 2k | 4k | 8k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| live room, vocal into the guitar's mic | 0.0 | -3.2 | -1.2 | -4.0 | -1.9 | -3.0 | -4.4 | -3.9 |
| treated room, the same | +0.4 | -3.8 | -1.4 | -7.4 | -3.3 | -6.3 | -7.2 | -7.1 |

The gobo shadows the direct path by 10-20 dB in the highs, but in a live room the bleed is
mostly the room's reverberation, which comes round and over it: a few dB. In the treated room
the direct path is most of the bleed and the gobo takes 7-8 dB off the mids and highs. The bass
bends round in both (nothing at 63 Hz). That is why studios treat the room *and* put up gobos.
The players' own spots hardly change (the gobo's reflection adds a little).
